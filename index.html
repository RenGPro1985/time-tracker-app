YTD Attendance & Year End Bonus

Year End Bonus estimate follows the standard 13th-month-pay rule: total basic salary earned during the year ÷ 12. Basic salary earned per employed calendar day = that day's applicable monthly rate ÷ the number of days in that calendar month; days outside the employment window (before hire / after termination) earn nothing. A rate change mid-month splits automatically at its effective date. Attendance (Days Present/PTO/PCH/PA/OUT) is tracked separately in this table and no longer affects the bonus calculation. Rendy identified Unhappy Day leave in the legacy tracker as OUT; the attached workbook is preserved for later reconciliation and is not imported in this build.
Choose a calendar year and run the report.

Active staff attendance

Total Working Days = Mon–Fri calendar dates in the selected year (to date for the current year), within each person's employment window. Days Present = distinct dates with any shift login or timesheet entry, even if no payable hours result. Admin-enter historical counts through Sep 27, 2026; automatic counts start Sep 28. OUT stays separate: scheduled day with zero payable hours and no PTO/PCH/PA marker. Year End Bonus = total basic salary earned this year (by employment days × applicable rate) ÷ 12, per DOLE 13th-month rules. Less Absences = Year End Bonus − (PA + OUT) × daily rate ÷ 12, where daily rate = current monthly rate ÷ 22; PTO/PCH are paid and not deducted.

PTO Credits

PTO starts after the 1-year anniversary, then +0.5 credit on every monthly anniversary date. Each weekday of an approved PTO request uses 1 credit, and pending requests are already taken out of Available. A year's credits expire after the last day of February the next year (Jan–Feb leave uses last year's credits first). Requests are whole days only, and staff can't submit more than they have available. Opening balances: earned is recalculated from the rule; used comes from the old tracker as of Oct 8, 2026.

Activity & billing rules

StatusBillableAllowance per instance (minutes)Notes
How pay is computed: every activity is billable except 60min Break, but only for time inside your shift window — time logged before your shift starts or after it ends is automatically non-billable. Select Overtime to bill time outside the shift window; Overtime has no cap and is always fully billable. Overtime started before your shift switches to Working automatically at your shift start. On top of that, minutes spent on a normal activity beyond its allowance are deducted from billable time (Overtime is never capped) — this is checked per instance (each time you use that activity), not summed across the shift. 15min Break allows up to 2 instances per shift (two uses less than 1hr apart count as one instance), 30min and 60min Break allow 1 instance per shift; any use beyond that limit is entirely non-billable. Personal, Bio and H2O Break have no instance limit. Set an allowance to 0 to never deduct.

Activities by client

Turn a break off and staff assigned to that client won't see it on their Activity screen. Working, Bio Break, H2O Break, Meeting/Coaching and Overtime are always on. Staff with no client see everything.
