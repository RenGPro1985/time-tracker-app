Activity & billing rules

StatusBillableAllowance per shift (minutes)Notes
How pay is computed: every activity is billable except 60min Break, but only for time inside your shift window — time logged before your shift starts or after it ends is automatically non-billable. Select Overtime to bill time outside the shift window; Overtime has no cap and is always fully billable. On top of that, minutes spent on a normal activity beyond its allowance in a shift are also deducted from billable time (Overtime is never capped). Set an allowance to 0 to never deduct.
